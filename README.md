# Uptown Funk AI

**Turn your photos into an Uptown Funk MV**

🌐 Website: [Uptown Funk AI](https://uptownfunkai.com/)

## About

Uptown Funk AI is a single-template music-video studio. The dance, street scene, camera movement and original soundtrack are already prepared. You upload a clear lead photo first, then optional supporting cast photos (up to 9 total), sign in, and create a roughly 15-second 480p 16:9 MP4.

## Highlights

- Create an Uptown Funk AI music video with your own cast. Upload photos and make a personalized MV with the original soundtrack.
- Uptown Funk AI turns cast photos into a ~15-second Uptown Funk music video with the original soundtrack. Upload 1–9 photos, no video or editing skills required. Create for $9.90 per MV (up to 255 credits) or use credit subscriptions and packs.

Visit [https://uptownfunkai.com/](https://uptownfunkai.com/) to try Uptown Funk AI in your browser — no install required.
